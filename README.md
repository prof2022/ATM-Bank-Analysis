# 📊 ATM Transactions Demographic Report Dashboard

![Demographic Dashboard](image.png)

## 📖 Overview

The **ATM Transactions Demographic Report Dashboard** provides in-depth insights into customer transaction behavior, categorized by **age group**, **transaction type**, **occupation**, and **state performance**. This interactive dashboard helps businesses and financial institutions analyze key trends in ATM usage and optimize their services for enhanced customer satisfaction.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 **Core Metrics**
- **Total Transactions Processed**: ₦38.56 billion processed across **2.1 million transactions**.
- **Utilization Rate**: Average ATM utilization rate is **12.9%**.
- **Unique Users**: **8,819 unique customers** performed transactions during the period.

### 📈 **Trend Analysis**
- **Daily Trends**:
  - Transactions peak between **3 PM to 7 PM** across most states.
  - Kano experiences an earlier peak around **11 AM**, while Lagos sustains activity beyond **7 PM**.
- **Monthly Trends**:
  - **March** saw the highest transaction volume and amount.
  - **February** had the lowest transaction activity.

### 📊 **Demographic Insights**
- **Age Group**:
  - **15-25 years** had the highest transaction frequency.
  - Surprisingly, **65+ years** outperformed some younger age groups like **46-55 years**.
- **Transaction Type**:
  - Withdrawals dominate across all age groups, contributing to **55%+** of total transactions.

### 📍 **State Performance**
- **Highest Utilization**: Kano (**18.6%**).
- **Lowest Utilization**: Federal Capital Territory (FCT) (**8.5%**).

### 🕒 **Transaction Durations**
- **Withdrawals** consistently have the longest transaction durations.
- Kano shows extended durations (>5 mins) for all transaction types compared to Rivers and Enugu

## 📸 Dashboard Insights (Snapshot Breakdown)

![Demographic Insights Snapshot](image.png)

### Key Observations:
1. **Age Group Patterns**:
   - Younger customers (15-25 years) are the most frequent ATM users.
   - Seniors (65+ years) exhibit significant engagement, requiring tailored services.
2. **Transaction Types**:
   - Withdrawals dominate ATM usage, but transfers and balance inquiries also hold notable shares.
3. **Geographic Efficiency**:
   - Rivers and Enugu provide the fastest transaction experiences compared to Kano and Lagos.

## 🌟 Impact of the Report

- **Customer Segmentation**:
  Businesses can tailor services to specific age groups and occupations for better customer retention.
- **Operational Efficiency**:
  Helps banks identify areas where transaction durations can be optimized.
- **Strategic Planning**:
  Insights into transaction types and customer behavior inform marketing and service delivery strategies.

---

## 🔍 Recommendations for Business Actions

### 1. **Improve ATM Utilization in Low-Performing Locations** (e.g., FCT)
- Relocate underutilized ATMs to more prominent, customer-accessible locations.
- Enhance visibility through better signage and landscaping.
- Offer promotional incentives like waived transaction fees or cashback rewards.

### 2. **Address Extended Transaction Durations in Kano**
- Increase the number of ATMs at high-traffic locations to reduce congestion.
- Upgrade machines to newer models for faster transactions.
- Regularly service and maintain ATMs to minimize downtime and technical issues.

### 3. **Encourage Alternative Banking Channels**
- Educate customers on online and mobile banking to reduce ATM congestion.
- Offer incentives for using alternative channels, such as cashback or reduced fees.
- Simplify and promote phone banking for balance inquiries and transfers.

### 4. **Optimize ATM Availability During Peak Hours**
- Schedule maintenance during off-peak hours (e.g., before 5 AM or after 8 PM).
- Ensure ATMs in high-traffic areas like Lagos are fully operational during evening hours.
